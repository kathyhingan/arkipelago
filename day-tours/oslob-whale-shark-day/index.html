Whale sharks,
the first session

A five o'clock pickup, the fast ferry across the Tanon Strait, and the first window in the water with the biggest fish in the sea, before the crowds and the heat. Back on Panglao by three.

DurationFull day, about 10 hours

The Day Tour

Across the strait,
before anyone else

The whale sharks of Oslob are the Philippines' most famous wildlife encounter, and the difference between a good day and a jammed one is the first session. We run the early crossing: the van down the Panglao coast in the dark, the fast ferry over while the strait is still flat, the briefing before the sun clears the ridge.

The municipal programme runs to a strict rulebook and so do we: thirty to forty-five minutes in the water, no touching, no flash, four metres of respect. A scuba ticket is available on the day for the same sharks from a quieter line.

One honesty note we give every guest: these sharks are fed, and that is the tradeoff of this site. In the April to October window, Honda Bay in Palawan holds wild, unfed whale sharks, and for a journey that can move seasons we will say so plainly and reroute you.

Panglao, Bohol
The ferry departs from Momo Beach

The Shape of the Day

Dark o'clock,
flat water, first session

05:00 · Pickup

The van collects you on Panglao in the dark, running bags and coffee, down the coast to Momo Beach.

07:00 · The crossing

The fast ferry across the Tanon Strait to Oslob, about two hours, the first session of the day booked ahead. Most of the boat is asleep before the sun comes up over Cebu.

09:00 · The encounter

Briefing, vests, then thirty to forty-five minutes in the water with the whale sharks. No touching, no flash, four metres of respect, enforced. A scuba ticket is available on the day.

11:00 · Tumalog Falls

Twenty minutes beyond Oslob, Tumalog's curtain drops over a moss wall into a shallow pool. The swim is optional; the cool mist after the salt water is not.

13:00 · Home

The boat back to Panglao, on the sand by three, naps on schedule. If the Coast Guard holds the crossing, the day moves to your checkout morning and nothing else changes.

The Practical Side

What is included,
what is not

Your logistics

Private van and driver on Bohol, fast ferry tickets across the Tanon Strait, and a licensed English-speaking guide with you the whole way.

Included

Hotel pickup and drop-off on Panglao, private van and driver, return fast ferry, whale shark registration and session fees, mask and snorkel, Tumalog Falls entry, drinking water, guide.

Not included

The scuba ticket (booked on the day), gratuities, lunch (a stop is built in), personal purchases, underwater camera rental.

The rules

The municipal programme enforces the rulebook and so does our guide: no touching, no flash, four metres, one thirty to forty-five minute session. Guests who break the rules are removed from the water; we would rather tell you here.

Weather and honesty

The encounter is guaranteed by the feeding programme. The crossing is weather. If the Coast Guard holds the boats, the day moves, and the rest of your stay does not.
