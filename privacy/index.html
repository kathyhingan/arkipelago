Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. Written in plain language, under Philippine law.

Effective date: 14 November 2026

The short version

Here is the whole policy in three sentences. When you write to us through the form on this site, we keep what you told us so we can reply and plan your journey. When you browse the site, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity measure how pages are used so we can make them better. We do not sell your information, and you can ask us at any time to show, correct, or delete what we hold.

Who we are

This website, luxurytoursphilippines.com, is run by Arkipelago Luxury Tours Philippines.

We are based in the Philippines, and Philippine law applies to what we do with your information, above all the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). The National Privacy Commission is the government agency that oversees that law. For anything in this policy, write to us at journey@luxurytoursphilippines.com.

What we collect

What you send us

The inquiry form on this site, and the short quiz that leads into it, ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, from any country
  • The type of journey you are considering (private, honeymoon, family, retiree, or corporate)
  • Your approximate travel dates
  • Your group size
  • Whatever you tell us in the message box: destinations, what matters most, anything on your mind
  • Your quiz answers: who the journey is for, what draws you to the Philippines, your pace and trip length, which regions call to you, what luxury means to you, and the traveller type the quiz assigns you

Each submission also carries three technical fields so we know where an inquiry came from: the address of the page the form sits on, a short source tag, and the date and time you submitted. Submissions are delivered through a Google form service (Google Apps Script) and land in our private inquiry spreadsheet.

What the site measures when you browse

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-FJDP6BJ7XF) counts visits and page views, how long pages are read, device and browser type, and approximate geographic region. It does not tell us your name.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how pages are used: where visitors click, scroll, and how far down a page they read. It shows us which parts of a page are working and which are not.
  • Like any website, ours is delivered by hosting infrastructure that processes standard technical details, such as your IP address, in order to serve pages.

What we do not collect

This site carries no advertising trackers, no chat widgets, and no social media pixels. We do not ask for passport details, payment card numbers, or health information through this website.

Why we collect it

  • To answer you. Every inquiry is read and answered personally, and we reply within 24 hours.
  • To plan your journey. Your dates, group, preferences, and messages are what we work from when we design proposals and itineraries.
  • To keep improving the site. Analytics tells us which destinations and journeys people are curious about, and where pages lose people.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Under the Data Privacy Act, we handle the information you send us on the strength of your consent: you chose to fill in the form. Where a journey follows, we use it to carry out the arrangements you asked for. Analytics runs on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used, using as little data as the tools allow.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity set small files called cookies in your browser. They hold a random visitor identifier between visits, which is how the tools tell one visit from another and how their reports stay consistent. These cookies persist until they expire or you clear them.

This site does not currently show a cookie banner. The only cookies it sets are the analytics cookies described above; there are no advertising or social media cookies. If that ever changes, this policy will say so.

You are always in control of your own browser. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and turn on its tracking protections. If you want to switch Google Analytics off specifically, Google publishes an official browser add-on for exactly that: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. Ever. We share it only with the services and people who help us run this:

  • Google. The inquiry form, the spreadsheet it lands in, and Google Analytics are all Google services. Google's own privacy policy governs how those platforms handle data on their infrastructure.
  • Microsoft. Clarity is a Microsoft service.
  • Google Fonts. The typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts, which sees the standard request your browser makes when it loads them.
  • Our hosting provider. The company that serves this website's pages to your browser.
  • Travel partners, when a booking follows. If you go ahead with a journey, we share the details your resorts, guides, transport, and activity partners need in order to actually run it. That is the point of the exercise.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Google and Microsoft process data in data centers around the world, which means information may be processed outside the Philippines. We rely on the contractual safeguards those companies put in place for exactly this reason.

How long we keep it

Inquiry details stay in our spreadsheet for as long as they are useful for planning and follow-up, and are then deleted or anonymized.

Analytics data is kept according to the settings of each tool.

Your rights

The Data Privacy Act gives you real rights over your personal data, and we take them literally:

  • To be informed. That is what this page is for.
  • To object to our processing of your data.
  • To access it. You can ask what we hold about you.
  • To rectify it. You can ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • To erase or block it. You can ask us to delete your data or stop using it.
  • To data portability, where it applies.
  • To be compensated for damages, where the law allows.

To use any of them, write to journey@luxurytoursphilippines.com. Tell us what you want us to do, and include enough detail for us to find your records and confirm it is you. We will act on your request as soon as practicable.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data or your request, you can take the matter to the National Privacy Commission: www.privacy.gov.ph.

Children

This site is written for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information through this site, tell us at journey@luxurytoursphilippines.com and we will delete it.

Keeping your information safe

Inquiry records sit in accounts with restricted access, limited to the people who plan and run journeys. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we can commit to is holding as little information as the work allows, on platforms with industry-standard protections, and keeping access narrow.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Material changes will be described in plain words, not buried.
