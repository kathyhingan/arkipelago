Our Commitment

A seedling for every day.
For every journey.
For the islands we love.

For every day of every private journey we book, we adopt one Philippine native seedling through the Haribon Foundation's Forests for Life Movement. Planted, tended and protected for three years. Because the beauty our guests travel for should still be here long after they fly home.

The Promise

Ten days of travel.
Ten trees in the ground.

Arkipelago designs private journeys across this archipelago, and every one of them now gives something back. From the first day we book to the last, each day of travel carries one adopted native seedling, planted in a restoration site and cared for by the communities who live beside it.

We chose rainforestation through the Haribon Foundation because it plants the trees that actually belong here: narra, lauan, kamagong and their neighbours, the species that once covered these islands. Native trees feed native wildlife, hold water in the mountains, and stand a far better chance of surviving than any fast-growing filler ever planted.

This is not a campaign we will retire next season. It is how Arkipelago runs from here forward, and it grows with every journey we are trusted with.

A twelve-day honeymoon plants twelve trees. A three-generation island crossing plants one for every day of it. The math is deliberately simple so it never becomes a footnote: every day a journey runs, one more native tree goes into the ground with three years of care behind it.

"The Philippines we share with our guests should outlast the journey."

Why Forests

The islands our guests fall in love with
deserve more than a postcard.

The Philippines is one of the most biodiverse countries on earth, and much of its original forest is gone. What remains needs hands, and ours are now in.

01
Native Species Only

Rainforestation plants the trees that evolved on these islands, the ones that feed wildlife, steady the watersheds and survive the storms. Nothing exotic, nothing for show. The forest that returns is the forest that was always meant to be here.

02
Communities at the Centre

Partner People's Organizations grow the seedlings, plant them and tend the sites, and earn a livelihood doing it. Every seedling supports a household as well as a habitat, which is exactly why the trees survive.

03
Guests Can See It

Travel with us and the story is never abstract. Ask us about visiting a nursery or meeting the community that tends your seedlings, and we will build it into the journey wherever the season allows.

Plant With Us

Would you like to
plant one yourself?

Many of our guests ask to go one step further and put their hands in the soil. Community planting days run with the rainy season, roughly June to November, and partner nurseries welcome visitors across more of the year. We are working with Haribon and their partner communities to open planting days near our journeys in Palawan, Bohol, Cebu, Siargao and the Bukidnon highlands.

Tell us at the start of planning and we will find out what is possible for your dates. Where a planting day is not possible, your journey still plants its seedlings, and we can arrange for you to meet the people who tend them.
