How It Works

One weekend. One destination. Ten travellers, at most.

Every departure starts from Calamba, Laguna on Friday afternoon, with pickup available along the way at the closest expressway exit to you, and closes Sunday afternoon, back where you started. Between those two points, the destination changes, but the standard doesn't: a guide who lives there, a group small enough that nobody gets left behind on the trail, and a pace built around the place, not a checklist.

Starting Point & Deposit

Every departure starts from Calamba, Laguna on Friday afternoon. Joiners coming from along the way can be picked up at the closest expressway exit to them, SLEX and STAR Tollway included, rather than travelling to Calamba first. Exact pickup points and times are confirmed once your seat is reserved. From there, transport to the destination, whether by van, ferry, or connecting flight, is arranged as part of your seat.

A deposit of PHP 1,000 per person holds your seat. Booking for any weekend closes the Wednesday before the week of departure, to give us time to confirm the group and lock in transport and accommodation.

Ten seats. One weekend. Wherever we're going next.

Tell us which weekend calls to you, and we'll hold your seat with a PHP 1,000 deposit while the rest of the group fills in around you.
